# Put a Safe to Your Digital Life

There are certain things at home that you would never casually leave on the living room coffee table.

Property deeds, passports, bankbooks, insurance contracts, family medical reports — you usually store these documents in a fixed spot, preferably a locked drawer or safe. Not because they are "something to hide", but because they are far too important to be flipped through by anyone.

But what about those equally critical digital files on your phone and computer?

Photos of your ID card, screenshots of bank card numbers, password notes, private chat logs, business confidential materials from work — they may be scattered across your photo album, notes app, WeChat favorites, or a folder in some cloud drive.

You have a safe for your physical life. What about your digital life?

## Why Ordinary Storage Is Not Enough

You might say: "My phone has a screen lock, and my cloud drive has two-factor authentication. Isn’t that secure enough?"

True, these safeguards block most casual snooping risks. But think carefully: they mainly protect the **entry point** — keeping others from grabbing your phone or logging into your account.

The question is: once data enters the storage system, what state is it in inside the system?

For most cloud drives and phone storage, the answer is: **stored in plaintext**.
That means your files exist on the disk as readable data. Security relies entirely on the lock on the "door". If the door is opened — whether through vulnerabilities, insiders, or legal requests — everything inside is fully exposed.

It’s like a safe with a lock only on the door, but transparent walls.

## DoraBox’s Philosophy: Encryption Is Not an Option; It Is Default

DoraBox does something many think is obvious yet rarely implemented: **all data entering the box is encrypted by default**.

You don’t need to tick an "enable encryption" checkbox, nor set a separate encryption password. Encryption happens automatically the moment data is written. Encryption keys are generated and stored locally on your device, never uploaded to any server.

What does this mean?
Even if someone gets hold of your stored files — whether from your device or the cloud — they will only see a jumble of unreadable random bytes. Without your key, this data is nothing but digital garbage.

**It protects not just the entry point, but the data itself.**

## What Should Go Inside the "Box"

Practically speaking, these types of digital files are worth storing in DoraBox:
**Identity documents.** Photos of both sides of your ID card, scanned passports, driver’s license photos, household register pages. If leaked, these carry a high risk of identity theft, and it is extremely hard to prove the fraud was not committed by you.

**Financial information.** Bank card numbers, the three-digit security code on the back of credit cards, investment account details, tax records. These are directly tied to your money.

**Passwords and keys.** Password notes for various services, SSH keys, crypto seed phrases. These are master keys granting access to your other digital assets.

**Private records.** Diaries, personal notes, medical records, legal consultation documents. These are your most private digital traces.

**Work confidential materials.** Business plans, unreleased product designs, client data, contract drafts. Leaks of these may directly harm your career and business interests.

You don’t need to dump every file into the box. Vacation photos, public documents and ordinary notes don’t require encryption. But for files that would cause serious trouble if exposed, the extra layer of protection DoraBox offers is well worth it.

## How It Compares to Other Solutions

**Vs. built-in phone encryption:**
Phone encryption protects the whole device. Once unlocked, all files become accessible. DoraBox delivers file-level encryption: even if your phone is unlocked, files remain secure unless the box itself is unlocked.

**Vs. regular cloud drive encryption:**
Most cloud drive "encryption" consists of transport encryption (HTTPS) plus server-side encryption. The platform holds the keys and can view your files. DoraBox uses client-side encryption: you hold the keys, and the platform cannot see your data.

**Vs. dedicated encryption software:**
Tools like PGP and VeraCrypt are powerful, yet steep in learning curve; average users struggle to keep using them. DoraBox strikes a balance between security and usability — its operation feels similar to a regular file manager, with professional-grade underlying security.

## An Overlooked Feature: Isolated Management of Private Data

DoraBox is more than encryption. It handles another vital task: **physically separating private data from regular data.**

Your phone album mixes holiday photos with ID pictures; your notes app mixes shopping lists with bank passwords. This mixed storage is itself a security hazard. When you hand your phone over to show photos or cast notes onto a screen, sensitive information may accidentally be seen.

DoraBox provides an independent, encrypted storage space. Private files live only inside the box and will not be mixed with ordinary files. You need a separate unlock to view them; at all other times, they stay quietly out of sight.
