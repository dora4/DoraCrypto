### Five Hidden Risks You May Not Have Considered

**Risk 1: Platforms scan your files**
Major cloud services run content reviews and feature analysis on every file you upload. Your photos may be used to train image recognition models, and your documents may feed natural language processing algorithms.
You will never be notified, nor given any choice.

**Risk 2: Single point of failure**
Store all your data on one platform, and you risk losing everything instantly if the service shuts down, suffers a data breach, or your account gets banned.

Multiple mid-sized cloud storage providers announced shutdowns in 2026. Users only received a 30-day notice to download all their data. Can you finish downloading 200 GB within just one month?

**Risk 3: Cross-border data jurisdiction**
Files saved on overseas platforms fall under the laws of that country. The U.S. CLOUD Act allows law enforcement agencies to seize data stored on U.S.-based servers, regardless of the user’s nationality.

**Risk 4: Sensitive files mixed with ordinary content**
Your ID photos, bank card details and medical test results are likely mixed with food delivery screenshots and memes. If your account is hacked, attackers do not need to hunt for a separate privacy partition — because you never created one.

**Risk 5: Unresolved digital inheritance**
If something unexpected happens to you, will your family ever locate your key files across dozens of platforms? Do they know your passwords? Do they have legal access to your accounts?
