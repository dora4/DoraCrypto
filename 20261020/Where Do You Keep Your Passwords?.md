Robots are entering daily life, yet rules to protect ordinary people have not caught up.

The Securities Association of China recently added a rule to industry standards: analysts are prohibited from directly adopting unverified data generated by AI. This comes after AI fabricated policy information and fictional industry data, which were released to the market under analysts’ signatures.

AI can help you get work done. But when things go wrong, you take the blame.

## DoraBox: A Physically Isolated Safe for Truly Sensitive Data

The difference between DoraBox and DoraCloud can be summed up in one sentence:
DoraCloud is your secure warehouse; DoraBox is the password-locked safe inside that warehouse.

**AES‑256 Encryption** — the encryption standard widely adopted by militaries and financial institutions worldwide. Brute-forcing it with current computing power would take longer than the age of the universe. Mathematically, your data is secure.

**Local Encryption, Keys Stay in Your Hands** — files are encrypted on your device before upload. Encryption keys remain only on your device and never pass through any server. Even if the server is breached, attackers only get unreadable ciphertext.

**Dual-Partition Isolation** — the regular zone and privacy zone use separate encryption policies and access paths. Store password lists, private keys and ID scans in the privacy zone; daily files go in the regular zone. A breach in one zone leaves the other completely unaffected.

**Camouflaged Entry** — to outside view, it may look like an ordinary calculator or notepad app. Only after entering a specific password or gesture can you access the real encrypted space. Even if someone around you picks up your phone, they will have no idea what this app does.

**Emergency Data Wipe** — one-click permanent deletion of all encrypted data in extreme scenarios, with no recovery possible. Hopefully you will never need this feature, but it has to be there.
