# Your Data Is Being Watched "Legally": A Sober Reflection on Personal Cloud Storage Security

## A Set of Alarming Statistics

In August 2026, the Cyberspace Administration of China released the latest results of its special campaign for personal information protection:

- A total of more than 20,000 apps and SDKs were inspected
- Over 4,000 were urged to complete compliance rectification
- More than 1,100 products with illegal and irregular practices were publicly notified
- Over 400 were removed from app stores
- More than 90,000 enterprises and institutions in the finance, education, healthcare, transportation and other sectors were investigated, with over 12,000 hidden risks identified and rectified.

Meanwhile, at the roundtable forum on *Personal Information Protection in the Age of Artificial Intelligence*, multiple legal scholars and industry professionals delivered a consistent message: AI is reshaping the underlying logic of privacy risks.

Zhi Zhenfeng, researcher at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, pointed out: Privacy risks in the past stemmed from database breaches. Today, large language models embed personal information into model parameters, creating technical barriers to the right to erasure. Professor Wang Limei from China University of Political Science and Law added: Re-identification technology keeps advancing, raising the bar for anonymization standards. Desensitized data may be restored into identifiable personal information at any time.

In short, traditional privacy protection measures are being gradually undermined by AI capabilities.

What does this mean? It means where and how you store your data matters more than ever before.

## What Kind of "House" Does Your Data Live In?

We can draw an analogy for mainstream data storage options available today:

表格

| Storage Method | Analogy | Risk Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local phone storage | A wallet carried on your person | Risk of loss or damage |
| Mainstream public cloud | An apartment rented in the city center | High — service providers have access; data leakage incidents occur frequently |
| Home NAS storage | A safe in your own house | Low — requires technical know-how and hardware investment |
| Decentralized / encrypted cloud storage | Files split across multiple anonymous safes | Low — eliminates single-point risks at the architectural level |

Most people currently rely on the first two options: storing everything on their phone, or uploading files to a major vendor’s cloud service.

The risks of phone storage are straightforward: loss, physical damage or theft. The risks of public cloud storage are more concealed. Nominally, your data belongs to you, yet the service provider holds physical access. When required by law to assist investigations, when internal staff act improperly, or when systems suffer cyberattacks — your data security hinges on the provider’s defense capacity and business ethics.

The reality in 2026 is that neither option is entirely reliable.

## Dora Cloud: Returning the Keys to Your Data

Dora Cloud is a cloud storage tool focused on storing private sensitive data and ordinary files. Its core positioning is not to offer larger capacity, faster speeds or lower prices. Instead, it addresses a more fundamental question: who controls your data?

### Architecture: Restoring Data Sovereignty to Users

Dora Cloud adopts a client-side encryption architecture. Files are encrypted on your device before upload, and encryption keys remain solely in your possession. This means even the operator of Dora Cloud cannot read the content of your files.

This is fundamentally different from mainstream public cloud services. In conventional cloud platforms, encryption takes place on the server side, and the service provider holds the keys. When external authorities demand data handover, providers have both the capability and obligation to comply. Under Dora Cloud’s architecture, such compliance becomes technically impossible — because the operator has no means to decrypt user data.

### Features: More Than Just Privacy

Privacy alone is not enough. A qualified cloud storage tool must also deliver practical functionality:

- **Categorized management**: Dora Cloud separates stored content into two tiers: "private data" and "regular files". Private data is protected by stronger encryption and access controls, while regular files support flexible sharing and collaboration without compromising baseline security.
- **Cross-platform synchronization**: Seamless sync across phones, tablets and computers. Photos taken on your phone and documents edited on PC can be accessed from any device at any time.
- **Offline availability**: Important files can be cached locally for access without an internet connection, which proves especially useful for business trips and travel.
- **File version history**: Accidentally deleted or modified files can be rolled back to earlier versions.

### User Experience: Low Barrier, No Complex Setup

Dora Cloud does not require users to understand symmetric encryption or zero-knowledge proofs. These complex cryptographic mechanisms are encapsulated behind a clean interface. You operate it just like an ordinary cloud drive: drag and drop files, create folders, share items with contacts. Encryption and security work automatically in the background.

This marks the biggest difference between Dora Cloud and many privacy tools built for tech enthusiasts: it is built for ordinary users, not security specialists.

## Three Real-World Use Cases

**Scenario 1: Switching to a new phone.** Migrating local data from an old device carries substantial exposure risks. If your important files are stored on Dora Cloud, you can retrieve all data simply by logging into your account on the new phone. You can then format the old phone with peace of mind.

**Scenario 2: Sharing work documents.** The common practice of sending attachments via WeChat or email routes files through Tencent and telecom operators’ servers, and copies persist permanently in chat history. Dora Cloud’s sharing function generates encrypted links with configurable expiry dates and access passwords; shared access automatically expires once the set time elapses.

**Scenario 3: Storing sensitive personal information.** ID photos, bank card details, contracts and medical records stored in phone albums or regular cloud drives can suffer catastrophic leaks if an account is compromised. Dora Cloud’s private data tier provides an independent encrypted storage space for these documents.
