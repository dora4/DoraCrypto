# Nobel Prize in Chemistry Announced Today: Scientists Spend Decades Studying Molecules; You Spend Seconds Deciding Where to Store Your Files

October 7, 2026. The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded this year’s Nobel Prize in Chemistry to Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai, recognizing their contributions to asymmetric organic synthesis — specifically, their discovery of nonlinear effects and autocatalysis in chemical reactions.

Simply put: they spent decades unravelling an extraordinarily precise molecular reaction process.

On that same day, you dragged a scanned contract into cloud storage, taking roughly two seconds. You never stopped to wonder which server the file lands on, who can view it, or how it is protected.

There lies an interesting contrast between how scientists treat molecules and how you treat your data.

## How "Precise" Are Your Files?

Let’s look at your digital life from another angle.
Your computer may hold: Word documents of work contracts, Excel client quotations, PDFs of last year’s tax filings, scanned property deeds, photos of your child’s birth certificate, and your family’s medical examination reports.

These files are no less "delicate" than a chemistry experiment. Each has its own purpose, confidentiality level and usage scenario. Contracts contain commercial terms; quotations bear client relationships; tax records reveal personal finances; identity documents hold sensitive personal information.

Yet your method of storing them is most likely: select all, drag everything into a cloud drive, done.

It is like dumping all lab reagents — toxic or non-toxic, stable or flammable — into one single plastic bucket and labelling it simply "chemicals".

## How Long Is the 2026 Data Breach List?

So far this year, multiple large-scale data breaches have occurred worldwide. 3.7 million medical records were stolen from US firm CareCloud; South Korea’s five major banks faced simultaneous AI-powered hacker attacks; 2.8 million unencrypted personnel records of US military personnel at the Pentagon were exfiltrated.

Domestically, the Cyberspace Administration of China has conducted personal information audits on over 20,000 apps, with more than 4,000 ordered to rectify violations. Many cloud storage and file management tools reserve broad rights to access user data within their terms of service — citing reasons ranging from "content moderation", "security checks", to "service improvement".

Your files are not risk-free; you just haven’t been targeted yet.

## Segmented Storage: Organise Your Data Like a Chemist

Back to the Nobel Prize analogy. A fundamental rule in chemistry labs: chemicals with different properties must be stored separately. Flammables kept away from oxidants, acids isolated from bases, toxic substances locked up. This is not over-caution; it is standard operating procedure.

Your files deserve the same approach.

One core design principle of DoraCloud is **segmented storage** — separating regular files from private data, just like classified storage in a laboratory.

Ordinary files (photos, documents, videos) go into the general zone for easy daily syncing and sharing. Sensitive private data (ID documents, contracts, financial records, medical reports) resides in a private zone with independent encryption and access controls.

Simple as this logic sounds, most mainstream cloud drives do not implement it. They place all your files in one space protected under a single security policy. Your family photos and screenshots of your bank passwords share exactly the same security level.

Does that make sense?

## Client-Side Encryption: The Key Stays With You

Another core feature of DoraCloud is **client-side encryption**.

Before upload, your files are encrypted locally on your device. They remain ciphertext during transmission and while stored on servers. Decryption keys stay exclusively in your possession — DoraCloud servers hold no keys and cannot decrypt your data.

What does this mean? Even if servers are compromised, staff misuse their access, or third parties request data handover, all they get is unintelligible garbled text.

Traditional cloud drives operate on the model: *“I’ll keep it safe; trust me.”*
DoraCloud works differently: *“You lock it yourself; I only host it.”* The party you trust is fundamentally different.

## One Task Worth Doing Today

The Nobel Prize is awarded once a year, reminding us how far humanity has pushed the frontiers of knowledge. But you don’t need a Nobel Prize ceremony to remind you: your sensitive files deserve proper care.

Do one thing today: open your cloud drive and take stock of what you keep there. Pick out truly sensitive documents and check their current security status.

If the answer is “mixed with ordinary photos”, “no encryption at all”, “fully relying on the platform for protection” — it may be time to change your approach.

Not every task requires Nobel‑level precision. But your private data at least deserves a lock that belongs only to you.
