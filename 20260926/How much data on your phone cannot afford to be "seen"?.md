# How much data on your phone cannot afford to be "seen"?

## — From GPT-6’s cybersecurity capabilities, discussing ordinary people’s essential need for encryption

### Some unsettling news

On September 3, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra.

The most explosive announcement at its launch was not its ability to autonomously operate computers, write code or create PowerPoint slides — but that it scored **full marks** on the ExploitBench cybersecurity benchmark test.

What does a perfect score mean?
It means this AI model can **independently discover zero-day vulnerabilities**. Zero-day vulnerabilities are security flaws that software vendors have not yet detected or patched. Before GPT-6, only top-tier hackers and security researchers were capable of this.

Now an AI can do it.

This is certainly technological progress. But look at it from another angle: if an AI can find vulnerabilities, who can guarantee your data will not be exploited?

OpenAI itself admitted it has detected "autonomous evolution risks" in AI agents — including the ability to coordinate and launch cyberattacks.

In other words, **attack capabilities have been democratized.**

But what about defense?

### Encryption sounds distant, yet it is all around you

When many people hear the word "encryption", their first thought is: *I’m no secret agent; I don’t need this.*

What you may not realize is that you use encryption every single day without knowing it.

- Open your banking app — the small padlock 🔒 in the address bar stands for TLS encryption
- Make a payment via WeChat — the data behind the QR code is transmitted encrypted
- Fingerprint unlock on your phone — this is hardware-level encryption

Encryption is not something only seen in spy movies. It is infrastructure for modern digital life.

**The question is: have you encrypted your files, photos, password vaults and private notes?**

### What is the cost if your private data gets exposed?

Let us look at some real risks.

**Identity leakage:** If photos of your ID card, phone number and bank card details leak at the same time, scammers can do the following and more:

- Open new bank accounts posing as you
- Apply for online loans in your name
- Register shell companies for money laundering
- Targeted scams against your family (calls claiming "your family member is in trouble" become far more convincing with specific personal information)

**Privacy exposure:** If your private photos or chat records are made public —

- Damage to personal relationships is irreversible
- It may harm your career in the workplace
- Psychological harm often lasts longer than financial losses

**Business losses:** If you are an entrepreneur or freelancer —

- Leaked client contracts → competitors learn your pricing strategy in advance
- Leaked financial data → suppliers renegotiate pricing terms
- Leaked business plans → your ideas get copied and launched by others first

These are not merely hypothetical "what ifs". They happen every day. You just have not been targeted yet.

### Dora Box: A genuine lock for your digital safe deposit box

Dora Box is a privacy-focused encrypted storage product within the Dora product line. Its difference from Dora Cloud is this:
Dora Cloud handles **secure storage of files**, while Dora Box handles **encrypted protection of sensitive data**.

As an analogy: Dora Cloud is your warehouse, and Dora Box is the password-protected safe inside that warehouse.

#### Core capabilities

**1. Military-grade encryption algorithm**
Dora Box adopts the AES-256 encryption standard — the encryption level widely used by military and financial institutions worldwide. Theoretically, with current computing power, brute-forcing AES-256 would take longer than the age of the universe.

This is not meant to intimidate anyone. It is to show your data is secured mathematically.

**2. Local encryption; keys stay in your possession**
Files are encrypted on your device *before upload*. You hold the encryption key yourself. The key never passes through Dora Box servers and is not stored in the cloud.

This means: even if servers are breached, attackers only obtain ciphertext. Without your key, that ciphertext is nothing but random characters.

**3. Partition isolation: physical separation between regular and private files**
Dora Box supports dual partitions: a regular zone and a private zone. Store everyday files in the regular zone, and truly sensitive items in the private zone:

- Password vaults for all your accounts
- Private keys, mnemonic phrases and digital asset credentials
- Scanned personal identity documents
- Medical records and financial documents
- Anything that would cause serious harm if viewed by others

The two partitions use separate encryption policies and access pathways. A compromise in one partition will not affect the other at all.

**4. Camouflaged entry + emergency data wipe**
Dora Box features a camouflaged entry. To outside observers, it may look like an ordinary calculator or notepad app. Only after entering a specific password or gesture can you access the real encrypted space.

In extreme scenarios (such as coercion), one-tap emergency wipe is available: all encrypted data gets erased instantly and cannot be recovered.
