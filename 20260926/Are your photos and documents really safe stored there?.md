# Are your photos and documents really safe stored there?

**2026 Guide to Avoiding Pitfalls in Personal File Storage**

## First, a few real cases

**Case 1:** In September 2026, Montana’s Attorney General, together with attorneys general from 15 states, launched a security investigation into OpenAI, triggered by a data breach on the Hugging Face platform. What was leaked was no state secret, but ordinary users’ data — chat logs, uploaded files, and usage habits.

**Case 2:** This year, the EU classified ChatGPT as a "very large search engine" subject to the highest level of regulation under the Digital Services Act. One reason was compliance risks in how massive volumes of user data are stored and processed.

**Case 3:** China’s special campaign *"Clear the Web — Rectify Chaos in AI Applications"* has removed more than 5.61 million illegal and non-compliant pieces of content. A large portion involved unauthorized collection and overreach in the use of user data by platforms.

These three incidents seem unrelated, yet they all point to one core problem:
**Your data is not truly in your control.**

## What requirements should personal file storage meet?

I started looking into this seriously back in 2024 and made a checklist. If you are shopping for a reliable storage solution, use it as a reference:

### 5 non-negotiable baseline requirements

1. **Data Sovereignty:** Platforms must not scan, analyze your files, or use them for AI training or ad targeting.
2. **Encryption in Transit:** Full encryption during upload and download to block interception by third parties.
3. **Encryption at Rest:** Files are stored on servers in ciphertext. Even if the server is compromised, the raw content cannot be read directly.
4. **Portability:** You can fully export your data at any time, with no vendor lock-in.
5. **Cross-device Sync:** Access from phones, computers and tablets — without compromising security.

### Bonus features

- Separate storage partitions for sensitive private files and regular files
- File version history to guard against accidental deletion or edits
