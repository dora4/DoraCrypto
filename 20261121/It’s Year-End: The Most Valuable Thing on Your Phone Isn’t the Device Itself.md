The Lunar New Year is just 40 days away.

Losing your phone stings. Not because of the hardware—this is 2026, and a smartphone is just a consumer good; you can always buy a new one.

What truly hurts is the irreplaceable data stored inside.

Everything hinges on one critical factor: **whether your data is end-to-end encrypted.**

Anyone who picks up the phone—or buys the stolen device—can easily bypass the lock screen. Even without bypassing the lock, attackers can remove the storage chip and read all data with professional equipment with little technical difficulty.

The outcome: every photo, file, chat log and app record becomes fully accessible. Your ID photos, bank details, password notes and private conversations are completely exposed.

## Year-End Security Self-Checklist

This is not fear-mongering. As the year draws to a close, spend five minutes running this simple check.

1. **Go through your photo gallery**
   Look for ID scans, financial screenshots and private photos. Move all sensitive items into DoraBox, then delete the original copies from the gallery.
2. **Check your downloads folder**
   Tax documents, contracts, bills and payslips? Transfer them into DoraBox and erase the original files.
3. **Review your notes and memos**
   Delete any written passwords, bank account numbers and sensitive personal information. Store these details only inside DoraBox.
4. **Turn off unnecessary cloud sync**
   Never let your ID photos sit on third-party cloud servers. Disable automatic photo backup entirely, or at least exclude all sensitive albums from cloud synchronization.

Once you finish these four steps, even if you lose your phone, you will only lose the hardware and replaceable data. Your truly irreplaceable, private information stays locked away in an unbreakable digital safe.
