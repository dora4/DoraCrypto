## The Hidden Costs of Data

We tend to treat “cloud storage” as a service — pay a few dollars each month for a subscription, and the platform hosts and syncs your files. Few people, however, tally up the full picture of this arrangement.

**Explicit cost:** Subscription fees. A basic plan on one major cloud drive costs roughly 200 yuan per year for 100 GB. If you need more space — say 500 GB or 1 TB — prices jump to 400–800 yuan annually. It may sound cheap, but this is an ongoing expense, and rates keep rising year after year.

**Hidden cost #1: speed throttling penalties.** Free tiers cap speeds at mere tens of KB/s. Want to download your files back? You have to upgrade to a paid plan. You used your own bandwidth to upload files, then your own bandwidth again to download them — yet you must pay extra for decent download speeds. This logic would make no sense in any other industry.

**Hidden cost #2: surrender of data sovereignty.** Once you hand files over to a platform, the platform gains de facto control. It may scan your files (“for security checks”), analyze your usage behavior (“to improve services”), and hand over your data under legal demands without notifying you.

**Hidden cost #3: breach risk.** In 2026, global data breaches continue to accelerate. Personnel records for 2.8 million Pentagon service members were stolen unencrypted; South Korea’s five largest banks were hacked simultaneously by AI-powered attackers within 25 hours; CareCloud confirmed a leak of medical records belonging to 3.75 million patients. Behind every such incident lies an impairment of users’ data assets.

Add all these factors together, and it becomes clear: **the true price you pay for cloud storage is far higher than the annual subscription fee.**

## An Alternative Economics of Storage

DoraCloud is built around an economic question: **how can we make storage costs transparent while returning control of data to users?**

**Client-side encryption.** Files are encrypted on your device before upload. The server only sees ciphertext. Even if a breach occurs, attackers obtain nothing but unreadable encrypted data. This brings the hidden cost of breach risk close to zero.

**Partitioned management.** Work documents and family photos can reside in a regular partition for everyday access. Sensitive items such as ID scans, banking credentials and contracts go into a privacy partition — separately encrypted with independent access controls. You would not keep cash and property deeds in the same drawer; data deserves tiered storage too.

**Decentralized storage architecture.** No reliance on a single data center. Files are split into shards, encrypted and distributed across many nodes. No individual node can reconstruct the complete file. This solves a fundamental problem: **there is no single key that unlocks all locks.**

**Transparent pricing.** No speed throttling to force plan upgrades, no file scanning to serve targeted ads. What you pay for is pure storage space and security services.

## Data Storage Strategy in an Inflationary Environment

CPI numbers tell us one simple truth: money loses purchasing power. The same 100 yuan buys less today than it did a year ago.

Against this backdrop, cloud storage represents a recurring expense whose value proposition deserves re-evaluation. If a cloud provider raises prices every year, tightens speed limits, and broadens its privacy terms, your “true cost” is vastly greater than the subscription fee.

DoraCloud offers an alternative: **let storage return to its core purpose — you pay for space and security, rather than trading away privacy for convenience.**
