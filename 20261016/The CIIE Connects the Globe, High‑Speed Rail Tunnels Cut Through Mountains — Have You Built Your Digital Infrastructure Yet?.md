Yet one thing flies under the radar: **have you built your personal digital infrastructure?**

## How Much of Your Data on the Phone Is Left Unprotected?

Try this quick check. Open your phone’s file manager and browse what is stored there. Chances are you will find:

- Front and back photos of your ID card sitting in the photo album
- Scanned copies of your driver’s license in the “Files” folder
- Screenshots of bank card details in the screenshots directory
- PDF medical examination reports in your downloads folder
- Various account passwords jotted down in notes
- Seed phrases for crypto wallets saved in text memos

The importance of these items speaks for itself. But how are they stored right now? They reside on the same device as food delivery screenshots, short‑video cache files and memes, protected under the same security policy — which amounts to almost no protection at all.

**While physical infrastructure in the real world gets upgraded, your digital infrastructure remains nothing but an unfinished shell.**

## 8 Categories of Files That Belong in a Digital Safe

DoraBox is built for exactly this purpose: a digital safe dedicated to storing sensitive data. It is not a cloud disk, nor a plain file manager. Instead, it is an encrypted storage space accessible only to you.

Below is a checklist of files that most need locking. You do not need to tick every box; just a few will suffice:

**1. Identity documents.** Scans or photos of ID cards, passports, driver’s licenses and household registration booklets. Replacing them after loss is troublesome, and identity theft can be far worse. Identity fraud using leaked personal details remained rampant in 2026.

**2. Financial records.** Bank card numbers, insurance policies, investment records, tax documents and payslips. Leaks of this kind do not merely cause embarrassment; they carry direct risks of financial loss.

**3. Passwords and cryptographic keys.** Account passwords, crypto seed phrases, private keys and API keys. Back in September this year, a malicious app named FomoPeek quietly stole crypto wallet private keys via Apple’s App Store for 12 consecutive days — long enough to drain a mid‑sized wallet.

**4. Medical and health records.** Physical exam reports, medical histories, prescriptions and genetic test data. If insurers or employers obtain such information, it may affect your insurance premiums and career prospects.

**5. Legal documents.** Contracts, agreements, wills and property deeds. Your physical originals are locked away, and digital copies deserve the same level of protection.

**6. Private communications.** Screenshots of chats, letters and journals meant for your eyes only. Not because the content is problematic, but because they belong solely to you.

**7. Work confidential materials.** Business plans, client data, internal documents and unreleased technical materials. Even if your company runs its own confidentiality system, you should maintain an independent line of defence on your personal phone.

**8. Children’s personal information.** Photos, school records and medical files of minor children. When you make decisions for them, you bear the duty to safeguard their privacy.

## How DoraBox Protects Your Files

Three core technical features:

**AES‑256 Encryption.** This is the encryption standard adopted by global financial institutions and military bodies. Without the key, brute‑force cracking of encrypted files is infeasible under current computing power.

**Zero‑Knowledge Architecture.** Keys are generated and stored locally on your device and never uploaded to servers. Even the DoraBox team cannot open your safe. This is not just a promise; it is baked into the technical architecture.

**Decoy Vault.** You can set a secondary passcode. Entering it opens a fake vault holding trivial files. This serves extreme scenarios such as phone robbery or forced unlocking, letting you hand over the decoy vault instead of your real sensitive data.

Three highlights regarding user experience:

**Simplicity.** No cryptography knowledge or complicated configuration required. Launch the app, select files and tap to encrypt. That is all.

**Biometric authentication.** Unlock via fingerprint or face ID; no need to type passwords repeatedly for daily use. Your biometric data stays locally on your device.

**Standalone operation.** No reliance on internet access or cloud sync. Your vault lives on your phone and can be opened offline anytime.
