## How Much of What You Own Is Only “Borrowed Access”?

Follow this line of thought, and you may notice a quiet pattern in modern life.
More and more things that seem “yours” are, in reality, only things you are **allowed** to use.

- The songs you stream? Gone once your subscription lapses.
- The photos you took? Stored on cloud drives, subject to platform review.
- Your chat logs? Held on company servers, accessible to the platform.
- Your password manager? Tied to an app — what happens if the service gets acquired?
- Your medical test reports? Locked in a hospital system, inaccessible once you switch providers.

Every item comes with an unspoken asterisk.
And what comes after that asterisk means you do not hold full control over it.

## What Makes DoraBox Different?

Put simply, DoraBox adds a genuine safe to your phone.
Not the kind you open with a regular password —
it is a safe that **even its own makers cannot unlock**.

Here is how it works:
**Layer 1: AES-256 encryption.**
This is the standard adopted by militaries and financial institutions worldwide. Brute-forcing it with current computing power would take longer than the age of the universe. Your data is secured by mathematics.

**Layer 2: Local encryption, keys stay with you.**
Files are encrypted on your own device before upload. Only ciphertext leaves your phone. You hold the sole decryption key, and no keys are stored on DoraBox servers.

**Layer 3: Dual isolated partitions.**
Use the general zone for everyday files, and the privacy zone for highly sensitive content: password vaults, private keys, ID scans, financial records. The two spaces are fully separated; a compromise in one will not affect the other.

**Layer 4: Decoy entry.**
To outsiders, it may look like just a calculator or note app. Only a special passcode unlocks the hidden encrypted vault.

**Layer 5: Emergency data wipe.**
In urgent scenarios, one tap permanently erases all encrypted data, with no recovery option.
