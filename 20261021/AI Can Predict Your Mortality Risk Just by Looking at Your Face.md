## The Cascading Damage of a Single Data Leak

You may think: *I’m no celebrity; nobody would want my data.*

But you do not need to be famous. You only need to hold exploitable personal information.

**Identity breach → Precision-targeted fraud.**
When scammers know your full name, workplace, bank card last digits, and your latest medical test results, their calls are no longer crude scams like “you’ve won a million dollars.” Instead, they launch hyper-realistic targeted attacks that are nearly impossible to tell apart from genuine calls.

**Privacy exposure → Permanent harm.**
Once private photos leak online, they can never be fully erased. Copying and sharing data on the internet costs nothing, while erasing it forever costs infinitely much.

**Password leakage → A domino effect.**
Most people reuse identical or similar passwords across dozens of platforms. If one account gets breached, all others become vulnerable.

This is not a hypothetical “worst-case scenario.” It happens every single day. You just haven’t been targeted yet.
