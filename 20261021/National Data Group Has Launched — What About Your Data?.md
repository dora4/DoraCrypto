## An Unflattering Audit

Let’s run a quick audit. This is what your data storage landscape likely looks like:
**Local phone storage:** Files vanish when you switch devices. Data may be fully copied during repairs, with no way for you to verify.
**WeChat File Transfer Assistant:** Automatically purges files after 7 days. If you forget to back up critical documents, they are gone for good.
**Third-party cloud drives (Baidu / Alibaba):** Limited free storage and throttled download speeds. Worse still — platforms scan file contents for compliance reviews. You may think you are storing private files, but the platform knows exactly what is inside.
**iCloud:** Apple has acknowledged it analyzes user photos. Your family photo album sits on Apple’s servers; it is not “visible only to you.”
**In-app caches across apps:** ID photos you uploaded, personal information you filled out on services, stored on unknown servers. You cannot view, delete or migrate them.

An average person easily accumulates over 100GB of digital belongings. Yet few stop to ask: where exactly are these files, who administers them, and who can access them?

## The Gap Between “Storage” and “Secure Storage”

Most people assume uploading files to the cloud equals safe keeping.
This is a dangerous misconception.

**Uploading to the cloud ≠ secure storage.**
Truly secure storage needs to satisfy at minimum these requirements:

1. **End-to-end encryption:** Files are encrypted before leaving your device, remaining as ciphertext throughout transmission and storage
2. **Zero-knowledge architecture:** The service never holds decryption keys; even platform engineers cannot view your files
3. **Private partitions:** Sensitive files are logically and physically separated from regular files under independent encryption policies
4. **Data portability:** You can fully export your data anytime, free from platform lock-in
5. **Resistance to content scanning:** Platforms will not scan, flag or delete your personal files for content moderation

Take a look at the cloud service you are using. How many boxes does it tick?
