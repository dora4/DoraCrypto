## What DoraCloud Sets Out to Achieve

First, let’s be clear: DoraCloud is not just another cloud disk.

The business logic of traditional cloud disks is simple: *You hand over your files to us, and we store them.* Every file you upload enters the service provider’s data center, governed by their rules and managed by their employees. You only get access permissions, not ownership of your own data.

DoraCloud operates on a completely different principle. It is a **personal self-hosted cloud storage system**: your data stays fully under your control, while you can access it anytime, anywhere from any device—just like a regular cloud disk.

Sounds contradictory? It is not. The secret lies in its architecture.

DoraCloud adopts a **distributed node + end-to-end indexing** design. Your data is stored across storage nodes you personally select: home devices, private servers, or encrypted cloud space of your choice. Only the terminal in your possession manages the access index. In short: *you decide where your data physically resides, and only you can view its directory catalog.*

This design solves all three tiers of storage pain points:

- **Capacity**: Nodes can be scaled freely from dozens of GB up to tens of TB.
- **Smart retrieval**: AI semantic indexing supports natural language search. Type *“backlit photos taken in the park last autumn”*, and it pulls up all matching pictures, videos and screenshots instantly.
- **Ironclad security**: No third-party platform processes your data in plaintext. Not even DoraCloud’s own engineers can view your file content.
