#### Why "Separate Storage" Is the Most Important Habit to Adopt in 2026

Many people assume their data is safe as long as they use a cloud drive. The reality, however, is that most cloud drives store all files together, with coarse-grained permission management and opaque encryption mechanisms. If an account is compromised or the platform suffers an attack, all files may be stolen in bulk via database dumping.

People who truly understand data security have already adopted one practice: separation of data management.

- **Ordinary files**: Movies, variety shows, temporary work materials, publicly shareable documents. These can be stored on standard cloud drives for convenience, large storage capacity and fast sharing.
- **Sensitive files**: ID photos, contracts, financial records, medical reports, private chat logs, family footage, backups of critical accounts. These must reside in a dedicated space that is genuinely secure, controllable and encrypted.

This is much like how you would not pile cash, property deeds and old magazines into the same drawer. The same applies to files: sensitive data must be stored separately, encrypted separately, and managed with independent permissions.

We are not dismissing the value of regular cloud drives. Standard cloud storage works well for ordinary files — convenient, low-cost and fit for purpose.

Sensitive files are different. They carry your identity, assets, family privacy and life records. They deserve a more secure, controllable and professional storage solution.

Therefore, the smart approach is:

- Keep ordinary files on your everyday cloud drive for ease of use;
- Keep sensitive files on Dora Cloud for peace of mind.

Separate management is not a hassle — it is maturity.
Just as you would not hand your front-door key to a stranger, you should not dump all your files indiscriminately into one single cloud space.
